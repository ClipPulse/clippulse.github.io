ClipPulse

A free desktop app for creators: point it at your folders of finished videos, review each post once, and ClipPulse publishes them to your TikTok account at the times you choose.

Download for Windows

Your folders

Add folders with videos, an optional folder with cover images and an optional captions file.

Queue in order or at random

Pick where to start, the first publish time and the interval between posts.

You approve every post

Preview each video, choose who can view it, comments, duet and stitch, and the cover.

Runs on your computer

Your accounts, files and queue stay on your PC. There is no ClipPulse account or cloud storage.

How it works

  1. Download and start ClipPulse. It opens in your browser at localhost.
  2. Click Add TikTok account and sign in with TikTok to allow ClipPulse to publish videos you approve.
  3. Add folders with your videos. File names are used as video names; cover images and captions are matched by name.
  4. Add videos to the queue, open each one, review the settings and approve it.
  5. Keep ClipPulse running. Approved videos are published to your account at their scheduled time.
