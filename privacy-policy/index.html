ClipPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ClipPulse is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information ClipPulse uses and where it is kept.

Information ClipPulse uses

  • TikTok account information (via the user.info.basic scope): your account's open ID, username, display name and avatar, so the app can show which account you are posting to.
  • TikTok access tokens that let the app publish videos you approve (via the video.publish scope).
  • Your files and settings: the folders, videos, cover images, captions files and queue you set up in the app.

Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your computer, in the ClipPulse data folder (%APPDATA%\ClipPulse on Windows). ClipPulse has no user accounts and no cloud storage, and we do not receive copies of your videos, captions, queue or account information.

What is sent to TikTok

When a video you approved is published, ClipPulse sends the video file, its caption and the settings you chose (who can view it, comments, duet, stitch, content disclosure) directly from your computer to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API. TikTok's handling of that content is described in TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Token service

To sign in with TikTok, the app must exchange a one-time login code for access tokens using an application secret. So that this secret does not have to be included in the downloadable app, the exchange (and token refresh and revoke requests) goes through a small ClipPulse token service hosted on Cloudflare. It forwards the request to TikTok and returns TikTok's response to your computer. It does not store or log codes or tokens, and your videos never pass through it. Cloudflare processes these requests as our infrastructure provider.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies – neither in the app nor on this website.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any information.
  • The app does not access your TikTok data beyond the two scopes above.

Removing your data

  • In ClipPulse, click Disconnect next to an account to revoke its tokens and remove them from your computer.
  • You can also remove ClipPulse's access in the TikTok app under your account's security / app permissions settings.
  • To delete everything, quit ClipPulse and delete the %APPDATA%\ClipPulse folder and the app folder.

Children

ClipPulse is intended for users who are old enough to use TikTok under TikTok's terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
